Compare CBS Carbon Intensities

This notebook compares three methods to calculate CBS carbon intensities for each product.

  • divByDesired: Divide the total CBS GHG emissions by the desired CBS column sums (total expenditures according to CBS)
  • divByActual: Divide the total CBS GHG emissions by the actual CBS column sums after one RAS (total expenditures according to CBS scaled to EXIOBASE expenditures per product)
  • twoRAS: Divide the total CBS GHG emissions by the actual CBS column sums after two RAS. The first RAS results in the total expenditures according to CBS scaled to EXIOBASE expenditures per product. The second RAS then optimises the matrix (again) for EXIOBASE expenditures.

If the carbon intensity of a product is different in one method compared to the other, it indicates that

  • Carbon intensity is lower in divByActual and twoRAS than in divByDesired: EXIOBASE estimates higher expenditures than CBS
  • Carbon intensity is higher in divByActual and twoRAS than in divByDesired: EXIOBASE estimates smaller expenditures than CBS
